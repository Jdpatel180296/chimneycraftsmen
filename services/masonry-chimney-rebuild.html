Structural masonry specialists

Rebuild the stack instead of repeating temporary repairs

A leaning chimney, widespread spalling, open mortar joints, or severe water damage can compromise more than appearance. Our masonry team evaluates the existing stack, identifies salvageable areas, and recommends a partial or full rebuild based on condition and budget.

We match brick and stone as closely as practical, rebuild sound courses, and detail the crown and flashing so the finished chimney is ready for the next season of Southern California weather.

What a rebuild includes

  • Condition review and photo documentation
  • Safe removal of unstable masonry
  • Compatible brick, stone, and mortar selection
  • Rebuilt crown, joints, and weather details

Partial or complete rebuilds

We can focus on the crown and upper courses or coordinate a complete stack rebuild when the structure requires it.
