Repair the cause, not just the symptom

Clear answers for damaged chimneys

Los Angeles chimneys face heat, rain, seismic movement, and salty marine air. We inspect the visible masonry and the components that are easy to miss, then explain which repairs are urgent and which can be planned.

Our repair work can address loose brick, deteriorated mortar, crown cracks, firebox damage, flue issues, flashing failures, and partial or complete rebuilds. You receive a straightforward scope of work and documentation suitable for an insurance claim, refinance, or home sale.

Our repair process

  • On-site inspection and safety assessment
  • Photo documentation and written recommendations
  • Code-conscious repair plan and transparent estimate
  • Careful masonry work and clean final walkthrough
