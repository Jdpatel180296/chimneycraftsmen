Leak Prevention & Protection

Keep Los Angeles rain outside your chimney.

Waterproofing is more than applying a coating. We trace the entry point, repair weak details, and protect the masonry with the right combination of sealing, flashing, and caps.

Find the entry point

Water damage rarely starts where the stain appears

Chimney leaks can travel through cracked crowns, loose mortar, failed flashing, open caps, or porous masonry before showing up on a ceiling or wall. Our inspection looks at the full roof-to-fireplace path instead of masking one symptom.

Once the source is understood, we recommend only the work needed: compatible water repellent for sound masonry, crown or flashing repair where details have failed, and a properly fitted cap when the opening needs protection.

Waterproofing details that work together

Flashing

Repair roof-to-chimney transitions so water cannot bypass the masonry at the base.

Crown and cap

Close cracks at the top and keep rain, leaves, and animals out of the flue opening.

Masonry seal

Use an appropriate breathable treatment to reduce absorption without trapping moisture.
